### How we got the name

Facebook will not accept bare `Edgeways` (`Page names that are too broad
are not allowed`). That is an automated filter on **new** Pages, not a
judgement that the brand is weak. `edgeways` is also a real English word
("sideways, edge first"), so the bot treats it like Pulse or Anchor, not
like Airbnb (a coined, famous, verified mark).

**Do not try `EdgeWays`.** Unusual internal caps are in Meta's naming
rules and do not match the lockup or the prose. It will likely bounce, and
if it does not you are stuck with a spelling you never use.

Matched betting incumbents often put the domain in the Facebook name
(Outplayed is `OUTPLAYED.com`). That is a valid way past "too broad", and
it tells people where to go. Do not copy their all-caps, emoji, or
"make money online" bio, and do not switch category to Education.

Try Facebook names in this order:

1. `edgeways.app` — lowercase, matches the real domain and the lockup.
   Not `Edgeways.app`, not `EDGEWAYS.app`. If Meta rejects it as a URL,
   that is one try, then move on.
2. `Edgeways Desk` — Desk is what the product calls itself.
3. `Edgeways Software` — blander, very likely to pass.
4. `Edgeways App` — last resort.

The vanity URL can still be `@edgeways`. Instagram, X and the rest stay
**Edgeways**. After the Page exists, a UK IPO mark (EDGE-10) and Meta
verification make a later rename to `Edgeways` realistic. Do not spend the
evening retrying the single word.

Do not use EdgeDesk. Do not put ⚡ in the name. The bolt is the **avatar**
(`square.png`), not a character in the word.

On Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube the display name can stay
**Edgeways**. Only Facebook needs the extra word. The vanity URL can still
be `@edgeways` or `@edgewaysapp`.

**Casing (do not change the app for Facebook):**

| Surface | Form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lockup / logo | lowercase, drawn | `⚡edgeways` |
| In a sentence, legal, Page name | title case | Edgeways is the… |
| Domain / URL | always lowercase | `edgeways.app` |
| Facebook Page name | locked | `edgeways.app` |

Never write `Edgeways.app` in a sentence. That mixes a proper noun with a
TLD. The domain is `edgeways.app`. The product is Edgeways. The picture of
the name is lowercase. Those three can coexist. The marketing site already
does this: lowercase lockup, "Edgeways is the matched betting command
centre" in the hero. Keep it. Do not retitle the desk wordmark.

Using `edgeways.app` as the **Facebook Page name** is a filter workaround
(same move as Outplayed's `OUTPLAYED.com`), not a change to how you write
the brand elsewhere.

**Never pick** Gambling, Casino, Betting shop, or Bookmaker. That classifies
the Page as a gambling operator. Edgeways is software that tracks the user's
own bets. It does not take wagers.

**Categories (Facebook allows up to three):** The first tag is the **primary**.
That is the one that shows under the Page name. Extra tags help search and
appear in About. They do not stack as a list on the cover. Two is enough:
`Software` (what a stranger should read) then `App Page` (helps app search).
Do not add a third. Do not add Product/service, Internet company, or anything
betting-related. If the chips are already `App Page` + `Software`, remove and
re-add so **Software** is first, or leave it if Facebook will not reorder.
Either pair is fine; order is the only thing worth a 10-second tidy.

### Paid ads and targeting

You cannot cleanly target "matched bettors" as a Meta interest the way
2018 ads could. That checkbox is mostly gone. OddsMonkey being live means
**they** are an authorised advertiser, not that a new software Page skips
review. The first paid job is: will Meta approve an ad that says matched
betting at all.

**How matched bettors actually see the ad**

1. The **creative and landing page** say matched betting. Broad UK 18+
   traffic still self-selects. This does more than any interest list.
2. **Geo + age:** United Kingdom only. Minimum **18+**. Prefer **21+** or
   **25+** if Meta offers it. Never under 18.
3. **Advantage+ / broad** (Meta's current default). Let the pixel learn
   from waitlist signups. Do not stack twenty weak interests.
4. **If manual interests still exist**, use sport, not hustle: Premier
   League, horse racing, football, Betfair, sports betting. Skip "make
   money online", side hustle, crypto, payday.
5. **Custom audiences (the real targeting):** hashed waitlist emails;
   site visitors once a Meta pixel is on `edgeways.app`; then a lookalike
   of those lists once you have a few hundred signups.
6. You **cannot** target OddsMonkey users, Facebook Group members, or
   "people who do matched betting" as a first-party list you do not own.

**Do not**

- Promise profit, "risk-free", or income. ASA/CAP, and a licence-adjacent
  trigger (`docs/legal/gambling-licence-assessment.md` §5 and §7.5).
- Use Follow as the button. Sign up → `https://edgeways.app`.
- Boost the website OG card. Use `brand/social/facebook-feed-4x5.png`.
- Run ads from the personal profile.

**Order of spend**

1. Business Manager + ad account. Submit any gambling/gaming
   authorisation Meta asks for. Honest product line: subscription
   software that tracks the user's own bets; never takes a wager.
2. Meta pixel (and later CAPI) on the waitlist form. Conversion =
   waitlist submit, not Page likes.
3. A **£15–30/day** approval test for a few days. If rejected, appeal as
   software, not a bookmaker. Do not rewrite the ad into "make money".
4. Once approved: retarget visitors who did not join, then lookalikes.
5. Google Search for "matched betting tracker / spreadsheet" is higher
   intent than Facebook cold feed. Same claims rules. Do that when the
   landing is the launch variant, not only a waitlist.

Organic MB Facebook groups are a **post**, not an ad. One useful note
with a link. Not daily spam.

Not legal advice. A big earnings-claim push is the advertising-law
trigger, not a Gambling Commission one.

## 5. What to post (and what not to)

Until launch, the job is **look real + collect waitlist**. A quiet, finished
Page beats a half-dead feed.

**Do post**

- Waitlist is open. Link.
- A still of the desk (Do Next, not a fabricated P&L flex).
- Launch / beta timing when you have it ("weeks, not months" is already on the site).
- 18+ and BeGambleAware on the Page itself (About, or the pinned post).

**Do not post**

- Today's tips, odds, or "have this bet".
- "Risk-free profit", ROI screenshots as a promise, income-claim carousels.
- Anything aimed at under-18s, or any creative that looks like a bookmaker ad.
- Daily stories for the sake of the algorithm.

A major marketing push with earnings claims is an advertising-law trigger,
not a licensing trigger. See `docs/legal/gambling-licence-assessment.md` §5
and §7.5.
